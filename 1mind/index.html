03 · Ideal Customer Profile

Where a Superhuman pays for itself fastest.

The best fits have lots of high-intent buyers, a product that needs an expert to explain it, and too few experts to go around. Score any account with the tool on the right.

Firmographics

B2B tech and tech-enabled companies, ~500 to 10,000+ employees, $50M+ revenue. Mid-market deals land fast; enterprise deals set the logo and the ACV.

Motion

Meaningful inbound or PLG volume plus a product complex enough to need an SE. Technical SaaS, data/infra, security, fintech, IoT, vertical SaaS.

Budget

Can support a six-figure annual platform investment. The strongest cases are funded from the headcount line (SDR/SE/CSM) or from Drift/Qualified renewal budgets.

Tech stack

Salesforce or HubSpot, Marketo, Salesloft or Outreach, Gong, Gainsight. Today's front door is a Drift or Qualified bot, a chatbot, or a form plus a scheduling router.

Triggers

Drift sunset notice · Qualified renewal after the acquisition · SE:AE ratio worse than 1:3 · new product launch · PLG conversion flat · SDR/SE hiring freeze · new CRO or CMO · international expansion · board AI mandate

Deprioritize

Budgets under ~$100K, outbound-only needs (pair with an AI SDR instead), low-traffic sites with no PLG funnel, and very simple products where a form works fine.

04 · Macro Sales Plays

Six market forces pulling buyers toward 1mind.

These plays come from the market, not a single account. Each one opens a door into hundreds of accounts at the same time. For each: the signal, why it matters, and how I'd run it.

Time-bound · Highest priority

The Drift sunset

Signal: Clari + Salesloft is retiring Drift and refers customers exclusively to 1mind.5 Why it matters: Thousands of B2B websites must replace their front door, and the evaluation is already happening.

How I'd run it
Build a Drift install-base list, co-sell with Salesloft AEs through a joint account-mapping cadence, and position the move as an upgrade from chat to a Superhuman, not a migration.
Competitive window

Qualified is now Salesforce

Signal: Salesforce closed its $1.2B Qualified deal on Apr 1, 2026 and is folding Piper into Agentforce.6 Why it matters: HubSpot, Marketo and multi-CRM companies no longer have a neutral option. Salesforce companies face bundling and roadmap uncertainty.

How I'd run it
Target Qualified customers ahead of renewal. Lead with a CRM-agnostic, video-first platform that covers the full lifecycle, where Piper only covers inbound.
Buyer behavior

The rep-free buyer, who still wants an expert

Signal: 67% of B2B buyers now prefer a rep-free experience, up from 61%, yet 69% still turn to reps to validate what AI tells them.12 Why it matters: Buyers want self-serve speed and expert validation at the same time.

How I'd run it
Open CMO conversations with the Gartner stats. A Superhuman is the only way to be rep-free and expert-led at once.
Capacity

The expert coverage gap

Signal: SEs are the scarcest resource in B2B sales. Mid-market and long-tail deals often get no SE at all. Why it matters: Deals stall mid-funnel and win rates drop where expertise is missing.

How I'd run it
Open with VP Sales Engineering, sell up to the CRO, and anchor on Experity's win rate going from 26% to 50%.
Budget

Headcount → AI budget reallocation

Signal: Boards want efficient growth, and SDR/SE hiring plans are being rewritten as AI budgets. Why it matters: 1mind should be priced against the headcount line, not the martech line.

How I'd run it
Build the CFO business case on capacity. HubSpot's Fiona did the work of ~80+ SDRs and 19 SEs. What would that capacity cost in salaries?
Product-led

PLG needs a closer

Signal: PLG companies have huge free-user bases and flat free-to-paid conversion. Why it matters: Activation is the leak, and human-assisted PLG doesn't scale.

How I'd run it
Lead with the In-Product Guide and 1mind's PLG calculator. Land on conversion, then expand to inbound and CS.

06b · Objection Handling

The hard questions, answered before they're asked.

Enterprise buyers will push on price, time to value, trust and brand risk. Here's how I'd handle each.

"Six figures is a lot for a website tool."
Reframe

It isn't a website tool. It replaces capacity. Compare it to the fully loaded cost of the SDRs and SEs whose work it covers, not to a chat widget.

Proof

HubSpot's Fiona did the work of ~80+ SDRs and 19 SEs. Run the value model in the next section with their numbers.

Close

"If we can show payback inside two quarters on your own inputs, is price still the blocker?"

"We can't trial it first?"
Reframe

A Superhuman is only as good as the content it's trained on, so a generic trial would undersell it. We earn the decision with a preview built on your content.

Offer

A mutual action plan with written success criteria (conversion, engagement, win rate) and a 90-day value review built into the contract.

"1–2 months to go live is too long."
Reframe

Compare it with the alternative: hiring and ramping one SE takes 6+ months. Implementation covers the persona workshop, content ingestion and avatar production, which is what makes it brand-safe.

Plan

Start implementation in parallel with procurement, and have a go-live date before signature.

"An AI avatar feels risky for our brand."
Reframe

The bigger brand risk is a buyer who waits three days for a reply, or gets stuck in a chatbot loop. Superhumans are built for brand fidelity, with the persona, tone and guardrails approved by your team.

Proof

HubSpot, LinkedIn and Nutanix put Superhumans in front of their buyers. Buyers engage: Fiona reaches 88% of the visitors who meet her.

"What about hallucinations and data security?"
Answer

SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 42001 (AI management), GDPR and CCPA. Customer data is never used to train models. Answers come from your approved content.

Action

Bring security in early. Send the trust package in week one, not week six.

"We already have Qualified / Drift."
Reframe

Drift is being retired, and Qualified is becoming part of Agentforce. Either way you have a decision to make. The question is whether to replace chat with chat, or chat with an expert.

Close

"Let's line the evaluation up with your renewal date so you're choosing, not reacting."

"Will this replace my team?"
Reframe

It changes what the team works on. SDRs work the accounts the Superhuman qualifies, SEs take the deals only they can win, and AEs spend their time on the relationship and the business case.

Proof

1mind's own AEs sell alongside Mindy. In one HubSpot deal, Mindy answered a 20+ person buying committee, and it closed in 11 days at $262K.8
