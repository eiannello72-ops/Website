01 My Read on 1mind

What I know going in

A short summary so we start from the same page.

Where Superhumans win

  • Website Inbound replaces chat and forms. HubSpot saw 78% more free trials.1
  • Ride-Along puts an SE on every call. Experity took its win rate from 26% to 50%.2
  • In-Product and Customer Success carry the same brain through renewal, which is where expansion comes from.

Who buys

  • B2B tech with 500 to 10,000+ employees, real inbound or product led volume and a technical product.
  • The CRO and CMO sign. VP Demand Gen or VP Sales Engineering champions. CISO and CFO approve.
  • The strongest deals are funded from SDR and SE headcount, not the martech budget.

Why now

  • CROs are being asked to grow pipeline without adding SDR or SE headcount.
  • Chat tools are being folded into CRM suites, like Salesforce buying Qualified, which leaves HubSpot and Marketo shops without a neutral option.3
  • 67% of buyers prefer to buy without a rep, yet 69% still want an expert to validate what AI tells them.4,5

03 Competition

How 1mind wins against each alternative

Where each competitor is strong, where 1mind wins, and a question to raise early in discovery.

Qualified

The incumbent website agent at many Salesforce companies, with strong reviews and deep Salesforce integration. Now being folded into Agentforce.3

High threat
Where they're strong
  • Large installed base and brand recognition
  • Native Salesforce workflows
  • Bundle pricing inside Agentforce
Where 1mind wins
  • Works with any CRM
  • Face, voice and live demo instead of chat
  • Covers calls, onboarding and CS, where Piper stops at the meeting
Question to raise

What has Salesforce committed to on Piper's standalone roadmap, and how does renewal pricing change if you don't expand Agentforce?

Intercom Fin

Shows up where Intercom already runs support, extending Fin into inbound qualification.8

Medium threat
Where they're strong
  • Already deployed, so expansion is easy
  • Capable text agent with flexible pricing
Where 1mind wins
  • Built to sell, not to deflect tickets
  • Video presence that builds trust in larger deals
  • Joins live sales calls
Question to raise

Do you want enterprise buyers talking to the same agent that handles password resets?

Docket

Positions itself as an AI sales engineer for text answers, RFP automation and website chat.9

Medium threat
Where they're strong
  • Internal knowledge and RFP use cases
  • Lower entry price
Where 1mind wins
  • Talks to the buyer on camera and on live calls
  • Far more enterprise proof
  • Covers the full lifecycle
Question to raise

Do you need a better search tool for your reps, or an SE your buyers can actually meet?

Demo platforms and AI SDRs

Navattic, Storylane and Walnut offer click-through tours. 11x and Artisan automate outbound. Both come up in AI for sales budget conversations.

Often complementary
Where they're strong
  • Fast to launch and cheaper
  • Good at top of funnel education and outbound volume
Where 1mind wins
  • Tours are one way. A Superhuman listens and adapts
  • AI SDRs create traffic. 1mind converts it
  • Sits in a different budget, so position it as a complement
Question to raise

When a buyer finishes your tour or clicks your outbound email and has a question, who answers it, and how fast?

Building in house

Engineering-led companies that plan to combine an LLM, retrieval and an avatar or voice API, or build on Agentforce.

Medium threat
Where it's strong
  • Full control
  • Looks cheaper at the start
Where 1mind wins
  • The hard part is sales judgment and brand safety, not the avatar
  • Security and AI certifications already in place
  • Live in one to two months instead of quarters of engineering time
Question to raise

Who will own accuracy, brand approvals and CRM sync once it's live, and what comes off the product roadmap to make room?

Hiring more people

The most common alternative. Add three SDRs and two SEs next year.

Most common
Where it's strong
  • Familiar and easy to budget
  • People still build relationships and close complex deals
Where 1mind wins
  • Hiring and ramp take months, and attrition resets the clock
  • Covers every hour, language and conversation at once
  • Frees people for the work only they can do
Question to raise

What is the loaded cost and ramp time of the next five hires, and how many buyers wait while they ramp?

06 First 90 Days

Built for an early team

At an early stage company, the first enterprise AEs build the playbook while carrying a number. This plan does both.

30days
First 30 Days

Intro to 1mind | Building the Foundation

  • Learn the product, company and market in depth through demo and call recordings, Mindy transcripts, customer stories and time with SEs
  • Sit with the founders and early sellers to capture how deals have been won so far, then turn that knowledge into documentation
  • Understand the competition and 1mind's biggest differentiators, and build battlecards for Qualified, Fin, Docket and in house builds
  • Talk to current customers about adoption, value realized and where they plan to expand
  • Audit the territory: open and dormant opportunities, customer lookalikes, vertical concentration, SDR hiring and chat tool signals
  • Build a custom account workspace for each Tier 1 account: business priorities, buying committee, pain hypothesis, proof points and openers
  • Create Tier 1 account POVs with the pain, stakeholder map, relevant customer proof and next best actions
  • Begin the playbook
    • Document TAM and ICP with a Tier 1, 2 and 3 prioritization matrix
    • Create the elevator pitch and value props by persona
    • Draft V1 messaging, call scripts and LinkedIn cadence
    • Align the ABM plan with marketing and SDRs
  • Launch the first self-sourced wave with SDRs, with an account hypothesis, persona messaging and a specific proof point for each account
  • Set daily and weekly syncs with sales, SEs and product
Visible output25 to 30 scored Tier 1 accounts, first meetings booked, V1 messaging, battlecards and a documented ICP
60days
First 60 Days

Creating the Playbook | Testing the Territory

  • Understand where deals succeed, where they stall and how to patch the holes in the bucket
  • Run ABM waves by vertical: data and infrastructure, security, fintech and vertical SaaS
  • Deploy the four plays as 5x5 ABM across enterprise and mid-market
    • A/B test messaging by persona and play
    • Omnichannel with a LinkedIn focus
    • Target ICP matrix: who to reach first, who to multithread to and when, and who to build as champion
  • Build the sales process where one doesn't exist yet
    • Triggers and pipeline qualification
    • Entry and exit criteria for each stage
    • Discovery and timeline expectations
    • Expansion triggers from the first use case to the next
  • Multithread every target account across the CRO, CMO, VP Demand Gen, VP Sales Engineering, RevOps, CISO and CFO
  • Use discovery to quantify pain: speed to lead, inbound that never gets worked, SE hours per deal and conversion by page
  • Build business case next steps: here is the problem, here is the impact, here is why 1mind, and here is who needs to be involved
  • Operating principle: one account, one hypothesis, multiple coordinated paths. SDRs create breadth, I create depth, SEs create technical fit
  • Turn meetings into MEDDPICC qualified opportunities with business cases and mutual action plans
  • Document the tool stack and SOPs so the next AE ramps faster
Visible outputTested messaging, qualified pipeline, stakeholder maps, MEDDPICC gaps identified and first business cases in flight
90days
First 90 Days

Playbook Deployment | Owning the Territory

  • Clear view of where 1mind wins, where it fits in the market and how to deploy the playbook
  • Account tiering and ICP prioritization complete, with outbound fully running alongside SDRs
  • Qualified deals advancing through preview, security review and business case with an executive sponsor
  • Every active deal multithreaded across the CRO, CMO, SE leadership, CISO and CFO
  • Forecast discipline on every deal: stage criteria, close plan, paper process and a dated next step
  • Pipeline built to close: no vanity meetings, no single threaded deals and no loose next steps
  • Quarterly ABM set and monitored with marketing
  • Field insights shared with product and leadership: feature requests, objections and competitive shifts
  • Steady presence on LinkedIn and in GTM communities as a voice for AI-Led Growth
  • V1 sales process documented, with weekly syncs on product updates and messaging
