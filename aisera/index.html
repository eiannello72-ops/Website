      Where each option is strong, where Aisera wins, and the question I would ask in discovery. Select an alternative to open its battlecard.

      ServiceNow and Moveworks

      Most common

      Where they are strong

      The system of record for IT service in many large enterprises, with Now Assist and Moveworks for employee AI.

      Where Aisera wins

      Agents that resolve across IT, HR, finance and customer service, with Automation Anywhere running the fix, priced for work done.

      The question I ask

      Of last quarter's tickets, how many closed with no human touch?

      Microsoft Copilot

      Bundled

      Where they are strong

      Already licensed, and inside Teams where employees work.

      Where Aisera wins

      Prebuilt domain agents with enterprise integrations and automation behind them, live without a build project.

      The question I ask

      Who builds and maintains each Copilot agent, and what does it connect to?

      Salesforce Agentforce

      Direct in CX

      Where they are strong

      Deep inside Service Cloud, with customer data close at hand.

      Where Aisera wins

      Employee and customer service on one platform, across any ITSM or CRM.

      The question I ask

      Do you want one agent platform for employees and customers, or two?

      UiPath

      Platform play

      Where they are strong

      A large automation installed base and its own agentic roadmap.

      Where Aisera wins

      Aisera's conversational agents in front of the automation, with the Process Reasoning Engine deciding the next action.

      How do employees trigger your automations today?

      Homegrown LLM builds

      Always present

      Where they are strong

      Full control and no new vendor.

      Where Aisera wins

      What will it cost to keep your build current as models change every quarter?

      Deal

      Where these deals stall

      Six stalls I plan for from the first meeting, and the MEDDPICC checklist every pilot and deal runs against.

      "ServiceNow already has AI"

      I compare what resolves end to end against what files a ticket, on their own top request types.

      The pilot never ends

      Ten request types, two systems, written success criteria and a readout date agreed up front.

      Security and data review

      I start the security package in discovery and scope integrations and permissions early.

      Two owners, IT and HR

      One business case with a line for each, and a champion in both.

      Seat savings need the renewal

      I map the ITSM renewal date and stage the seat retirement into the plan.

      "We'll build it on our own LLM"

      I price the integrations, guardrails and upkeep, against agents already resolving requests.

      MEDDPICC checklist

      Updated after every call and reviewed with my manager each week. Select a letter to flip it to the proof I need before the deal counts in my forecast.

      Commit ruleA deal enters commit only when the CIO has confirmed the metrics, the pilot has met written criteria, the paper process and renewal timing are dated in the mutual plan, and the champion has been tested.
