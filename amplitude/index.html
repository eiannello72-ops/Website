    Deal

    Where these deals stall

    Six stalls I plan for, and the MEDDPICC checklist.

    "Migration is too much work"

    Import their Mixpanel history and rebuild their top reports in the proof of concept.14

    Mid contract with the incumbent

    Start with experimentation or replay, and time the core move to the renewal.

    "Our warehouse already does this"

    Count the product questions in the data queue, then connect Amplitude to the warehouse.

    Product and marketing split the budget

    One business case with a line for each, and a champion in both.

    Security and data governance

    Security package and data residency answers sent in week one.

    A free tool in the stack

    Price the analyst hours behind GA4, beyond its license.

    MEDDPICC checklist

    Reviewed with Kevin each week. Flip a letter for the proof I need before a deal goes in the forecast.

    Commit ruleA deal enters commit only when the economic buyer has confirmed the metrics, the evaluation has met written criteria, security and procurement are dated in the mutual plan, and the champion has been tested.

    Why Amplitude

    Mixpanel sells strong charts, Pendo sells guides inside B2B apps, and PostHog sells to engineers. Amplitude puts analytics, experimentation, replay and guides on one behavioral dataset, then lets agents and builders work it from Claude, Cursor and Slack. As coding agents make shipping cheap, the platform that tells a team what to build is the one that matters, and that is an easy story to take into a Mixpanel or Pendo renewal.

    Your goal of every rep on the team past OTE is the standard I want to work under, and accounts sized by potential ARR are the kind I hunted from zero at a Series A startup.

    • Instrument a demo app, build a funnel, run a test, watch the Replay Agent and query it all from Claude through MCP
