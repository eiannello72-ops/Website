Warmer

The contact center gets managed as a cost line. It is the top of the revenue funnel, and every unanswered or unbooked call is a truck that rolls empty.

Deal

        
Where these deals stall

        
Six stalls I plan for, and the MEDDPICC checklist.

      

      

        
"Our customers want a person"
Let them hear recordings from their own market, and keep the warm transfer rules in their hands.

        
The pilot never ends
A few locations, written success criteria and a rollout decision date, agreed up front.

        
"ServiceTitan has this now"
Compare booked jobs on their own calls, side by side.

        
Brand by brand politics
One champion at the platform and one GM per pilot brand.

        
Security and call recording
Security package and consent language sent in week one.

        
Peak season freeze
Pilot in shoulder season so the rollout lands before summer.

      

      
MEDDPICC checklist

      
Reviewed with Joe each week. Flip a letter for the proof I need before a deal goes in the forecast.

      

      

        
        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      

      
Commit ruleA deal enters commit only when the economic buyer has confirmed the metrics, the pilot has met written criteria, security and the rollout plan are dated in the mutual plan, and the champion has been tested.
