    Warmer
    The North American enterprises I would target run New Relic, Datadog or Dynatrace agents across hundreds of services on Kubernetes. To hold the bill, APM gets sampled or switched off for part of the fleet, and seats get rationed.
    What we establish
    Partial coverageAPM on the services the budget allows
    Rationed seatsEngineers locked out until an incident
    
      
    Reframe
    The renewal price is set by the proprietary agent. Instrument once with OpenTelemetry and the backend competes on merit at every renewal, priced per signal with no seats.
    The assumption we challenge
    FromProprietary agents, per host and per user pricing
    ToOTel instrumentation, per signal pricing10
    
      
    Rational drowning
    The size of the problem, swapped for their own invoice in discovery.
    The data
    65%Name cost as the top criterion for choosing an observability tool12

    The CTO was asked to cut observability spend while AI assistants ship more code than ever. The SRE on call at 2 a.m. is looking at a sampled trace, and the service that broke had no APM at all.
    Why it matters personally
    BudgetA cut with no plan behind it
    On callBlind spots during the incident
    
      
    A new way
    OpenTelemetry through the Kubernetes operator, every signal priced per item, noise dropped by spam filters before billing, PromQL across metrics, logs and traces, and Agent0 tracing incidents to the commit.

    OpenOTel and PromQL, portable data5
