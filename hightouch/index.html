What I know about Hightouch

The product, the buyer, and why this year matters.

The product

Hightouch is an agentic marketing platform built on the Composable CDP. It started as reverse ETL, runs on the customer's own warehouse, and puts brand context, customer data and performance history in one place, so marketers build and ship campaigns end to end themselves.2

AI Decisioning adds agents that choose the message, channel and timing for each customer, and it is now the center of the company's roadmap.5

The buyer

The CMO or VP of Lifecycle Marketing signs, with the Head of Data as a second economic voice and a marketing ops or analytics engineering lead as champion.

Snowflake and Databricks account teams are natural allies, because Hightouch makes the warehouse more valuable.

The moment

Hightouch raised $150M at a $2.75B valuation in April 2026, with The Trade Desk's venture arm in the round.1

It passed $100M ARR growing over 100% a year two years running, with Domino's, Spotify, Aritzia, Cars.com, Ramp and PetSmart among its customers.1,2

The market Hightouch is reshaping

Packaged to composable

Packaged CDPs copy customer data into their own store. Composable CDPs run on the warehouse the company already owns, and Gartner named Hightouch a Leader in the 2026 Magic Quadrant for CDPs on its first appearance.3

Consolidation

Fivetran bought Census, Salesforce bundles Data Cloud into Marketing Cloud deals, and Segment sits inside Twilio. Hightouch is the independent, warehouse native option at enterprise scale.6

Agentic marketing

Budgets are moving from tools marketers operate to AI that decides and acts. AI Decisioning and campaign agents sit where that budget is going, and The Trade Desk's investment points to paid media and customer data converging.1

Where these deals stall, and what I do about it

Marketing and data disagree on ownership

I write success criteria with both teams in the room and make the Head of Data a joint owner of the outcome.

"We already pay for Data Cloud"

The business case counts the second copy of data, the engineering time and the renewal date, so the comparison covers total cost.

A pilot with no holdout

Every AI Decisioning pilot starts with a holdout group, so the lift is measured and nobody argues about it later.

Security worries about customer data

The data stays in their warehouse. I bring security in during discovery, and the review moves faster for it.

An agency owns the channels

I bring the agency in early as an ally. Better audiences make their work perform better too.

Budget is split across teams

The business case shows value for marketing and for data, and the close is timed to the CDP renewal and the fiscal year.
