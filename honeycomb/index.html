What I know about Honeycomb

The product, the buyer, and why this year matters.

The product

Honeycomb stores wide, high cardinality events and traces and queries them in seconds. BubbleUp compares slow requests with normal ones and surfaces the fields that differ.

It is built on OpenTelemetry, with SLOs, metrics and logs in the same place, and an MCP server that lets engineers and their coding agents query production.6

The buyer

The VP of Engineering, the head of platform or SRE, and the CTO, usually with a staff or principal engineer as the champion.

Finance and procurement join early, because observability is one of the largest and fastest growing lines in the infrastructure budget.

The moment

AI features are in production and behave differently on every run. Honeycomb launched Agent Observability to trace them.7

Palo Alto Networks now owns Chronosphere and Cisco owns Splunk, so many enterprises are reviewing their observability contracts this year.8

The conversation I would have with a VP of Engineering

Challenger meetings follow a set order. This is the talk track for Honeycomb, in my words, with the evidence I would bring to each step.

  1. Step 1Warmer
    Most engineering teams your size run three or four observability tools. And the first hour of a serious incident still goes to working out where the problem is.
  2. Step 2Reframe
    Dashboards answer the questions someone thought to ask in advance. Metrics are averaged before they are stored, so the detail that explains an outage is gone before anyone looks.
  3. Step 3Rational drowning
    Here is what that hour costs. We will swap these numbers for your own in discovery.
    $2Mper hour, median cost of a high impact outage91 hr to 5 minroot cause time for Intercom's Fin3
  4. Step 4Emotional impact
    Every chart on the incident bridge is green while customers can't check out. Engineers guess, roll back and hope, and the same people get paged again next week.
  5. Step 5A new way
    Teams that fixed this keep every field on every request, including build, customer and region, and ask new questions in the middle of the incident. The same data answers AI agents through MCP.
  6. Step 6Our solution
    Honeycomb, priced on events rather than hosts or custom metrics, so adding detail doesn't add cost.

Running the deal

Challenger earns the first meeting and shapes the problem. MEDDPICC tracks what is proven and what is still missing across a 6 to 12 month cycle at $500K to $1M+ ACV.

  1. Month 1

    Insight meeting

    Teach the reframe to the VP of Engineering and agree that slow incidents are worth fixing this year.

    Teach
  2. Months 1 to 3

    Discovery and business case

    Map tools, spend and incident history with a Solutions Architect. Price outage hours, engineer time and renewal cost.

    Tailor
  3. Months 3 to 6

    Proof of value

    OpenTelemetry data from one critical service, a real incident replayed, and success criteria in writing.

    Decision criteria
  4. Months 6 to 9

    Security and procurement

    Run in parallel, timed to the incumbent's renewal, with a cloud marketplace purchase raised early.

    Paper process
  5. Month 9 on

    Close and expand

    A multi-year agreement with a rollout plan, a value review date and the next teams mapped.

    Take control
