What I know about Honeycomb

The product, the buyer and the timing.

The product

Honeycomb stores wide events, each request with hundreds of fields, and queries them in seconds at high cardinality. BubbleUp compares slow requests with normal ones and surfaces the fields that differ.

It runs on OpenTelemetry, with SLOs, metrics, logs, Canvas and an MCP server that lets engineers and their coding agents query production.6

The buyer

The VP of Engineering, the head of platform or SRE, and the CTO, usually with a staff or principal engineer as the champion.

Finance and procurement join early, because observability is one of the largest lines in the infrastructure budget.

The moment

AI features are in production and behave differently on every run. Honeycomb launched Agent Observability to trace them.7

Palo Alto Networks now owns Chronosphere and Cisco owns Splunk, so many enterprises are reviewing observability contracts this year.8

The conversation I would have with a VP of Engineering

Challenger meetings follow a set order. Step through the talk track I would bring to a VP of Engineering, with the evidence behind each step.

Warmer

The engineering teams I would target run three or four observability tools, and the first hour of a serious incident goes to working out where the problem is.
What we establish
Tool sprawlDashboards, logs and APM, each seeing one slice
The first hourSpent locating the problem

Reframe

Dashboards answer the questions someone thought to ask in advance. Metrics get averaged before they are stored, so the detail that explains an outage is gone before anyone looks.
The assumption we challenge
FromWe need more dashboards and alerts
ToWe need to ask new questions of full detail data

Teams that fixed this keep every field on every request, including build, customer and region, and ask new questions in the middle of the incident. The same data answers AI agents through MCP and Canvas.

Where these deals stall

Six stalls I plan for from the first meeting, and the MEDDPICC checklist every proof of value and deal runs against.

"Our APM already does tracing"

I replay one of their incidents on high cardinality fields their APM drops or samples away.

The renewal was just signed

I start with one team or the AI workloads, and map the full renewal date into the plan.

Finance never hears about it

One critical service, one real incident replayed, and written success criteria agreed up front.

Instrumentation looks like work

Why Honeycomb

The big observability platforms grew up watching infrastructure. Datadog prices by host and custom metric, Dynatrace and New Relic automate root cause for failures they have seen before, and Grafana leaves a platform team to run the stack. Honeycomb was built for the question nobody planned for. Every request is kept as one wide event and priced per event, so engineers can ask production something new in the middle of an incident and get an answer in seconds, and Canvas and AI BubbleUp now do part of that work alongside them.

That is also why it travels. Engineers who learn to debug this way bring Honeycomb with them when they change companies, which turns today's customers into tomorrow's champions.
