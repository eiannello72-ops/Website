What I know about Ketch

The product, the buyer and the timing.

The product

Ketch captures consent and privacy choices, ties them to a person's identity, and pushes each decision into the systems that collect or use data.5

The Smart Tag serves Privacy Experiences with 400+ no code options, the Permission Vault holds every permit, and the Transponder carries it across 1,000+ integrations, now with the Agent Network on top.6,3,7

The buyer

The Chief Privacy Officer or General Counsel signs, with the CMO at the table, because Ketch gets to talk about revenue from advertising, data and AI, beyond risk avoidance.1

Privacy ops, marketing ops and data engineering run it, and security and procurement review it.

The moment

GDPR and CCPA made privacy a funded, board level requirement, and the wave of US state laws left enterprises managing a patchwork with tooling never built for it.1 California fined Tractor Supply $1.35M and Healthline $1.55M in 2025 over opt outs that never reached the systems using the data.8

AI sharpens it: models are only as trustworthy as the permissions underneath them, and the data a company holds is not always data it may use.1

Why Ketch

Legacy privacy tools stop at the cookie banner. Ketch governs how data is actually used across the whole stack, from the ad tag to the CRM to the model, and that is exactly what the 2025 settlements punished brands for missing. It also changes the conversation, because a seller gets to talk about revenue from advertising, analytics and AI, with a buyer that has mandatory budget and third party proof that shortens every deal.

With 30% of customers arriving from OneTrust, the Agent Network taking on assessment work, and a 65 person team that runs on passion, growth, grit, collaboration and integrity, Ketch has the product, the timing and the culture to win the enterprise.
