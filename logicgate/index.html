What I know about LogicGate

The product, the buyer, and why this year matters.

The product

Risk Cloud is a no code GRC platform with one data model for risks, controls, vendors, policies and issues, and applications for third party risk, enterprise risk, controls compliance, cyber risk, AI governance and more.

The Summer 2026 release made GRC Agents broadly available across third party risk, enterprise risk, AI governance and business continuity.4

The buyer

The Chief Risk Officer, Chief Information Security Officer, Chief Compliance Officer or Chief Audit Executive, usually with a GRC program lead as the champion.

Advisory firms shape many decisions, and LogicGate now has a joint business relationship with PwC.4

The moment

The move to LogicGate.ai marks the shift to an AI GRC platform, with agents built into the core.5 Gartner's first GRC Magic Quadrant since 2013 named LogicGate a Leader, and Forrester named it one of four Leaders in GRC platforms.1,2

Regulators are asking for more evidence, through the SEC's cyber disclosure rules in the US and OSFI's Guideline B-10 in Canada.6,7

Where these deals stall, and what I do about it

Every team wants its own requirements

I agree the first use case with the executive sponsor, then phase the rest into the rollout plan so scope doesn't stall the decision.

"ServiceNow can build it"

The business case prices developer time, change requests and time to value, so the comparison covers total cost.

An advisory firm shapes the RFP

I work with the advisor early, including PwC through LogicGate's joint business relationship, so the criteria reflect the customer's program.

No one owns cross functional risk

I map the CRO, CISO, compliance and audit early and find the executive who gains most from one connected program.

Migration looks risky

A solutions engineer scopes the data move during the demo stage, so the plan is part of the proposal.

The legacy renewal slips past

The incumbent's renewal and notice dates go into the mutual plan in discovery, and the close is timed to them.
