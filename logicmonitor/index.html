01 My read on LogicMonitor

What I know going in

A short summary so we start from the same page.

Where LogicMonitor wins

  • LM Envision gives one view across on-prem, cloud, network and edge, which is exactly where hybrid estates break.
  • Edwin AI turns alert storms into a single insight and acts on it. Forrester found 313% ROI with payback in six months or less.3
  • Catchpoint adds internet and digital experience monitoring, so teams see issues outside their own walls.1

Who buys

  • Enterprises with hybrid estates, large network footprints and customer facing digital services.
  • The CIO or VP of Infrastructure signs. The Director of IT Ops or NOC leader champions. Finance and security approve.
  • The strongest deals consolidate several tools and pay for themselves on retired licenses and reclaimed engineering time.

Why now

  • LogicMonitor passed $400M in ARR, and Edwin AI drives about a third of bookings.2
  • 84% of organizations are pursuing or considering observability tool consolidation.5
  • SolarWinds and VMware price resets are forcing IT leaders to rethink their stack.8,9

04 Sales plays

Four plays I would run first

Each play opens with a commercial insight, uses light relevance to earn the reply, and comes with a three email sequence.

Commercial insight

Outages rarely hide in one tool's blind spot. They hide in the gaps between tools, where nobody owns the full picture.

Target and signals

Enterprises running several monitoring tools. Signals include job posts listing SolarWinds, PRTG, Datadog or Splunk, a recent outage, or a new CIO.

Personas

Director of IT Ops first, then the VP of Infrastructure and the CIO.

Discovery
  • How many tools does it take to troubleshoot one incident today?
  • What did your last major outage cost, and where was it hiding?
  • When do your monitoring renewals come up?

05 Competition

How LogicMonitor wins against each alternative

Where each competitor is strong, where LogicMonitor wins, and the reframe I would use in discovery.

Datadog

Loved by developers and cloud teams, often already in the building for APM.

High threat
Where they're strong
  • Cloud native and APM depth
  • Strong developer adoption and brand
Where LogicMonitor wins
  • On-prem, network and hybrid depth with thousands of prebuilt integrations
  • Predictable pricing instead of usage driven surprises
  • Edwin AI across the whole estate, not just cloud
Reframe

How did last year's Datadog bill compare to the forecast, and how much of your on-prem network does it actually see?

Dynatrace

A Gartner leader, strongest in application centric enterprises.11

High threat
Where they're strong
  • Application performance and precise root cause
  • Deep executive relationships in large enterprises
Where LogicMonitor wins
  • Faster deployment and broader infrastructure and network coverage
  • Explainable AI that ops teams trust
  • Simpler to operate day to day
Reframe

How long did it take to reach full coverage, and who on your team can run it without a specialist?

SolarWinds

Entrenched in network monitoring, now private and moving customers to three year subscriptions.8

Displacement target
Where they're strong
  • Familiar to network teams
  • Deep install base and muscle memory
Where LogicMonitor wins
  • SaaS, with no servers or upgrades to maintain
  • AI built in instead of bolted on
  • Renewal resets of 100% to 300% make the comparison natural
Reframe

What does the new three year quote look like next to a SaaS platform you never have to patch?

Status quo

Zabbix, Nagios, PRTG and scripts that a few engineers keep alive.

Most common
Where it's strong
  • Low license cost
  • Full control and familiarity
Where LogicMonitor wins
  • Engineers go back to running infrastructure instead of maintaining monitoring
  • Coverage, AI and support that homegrown tools can't match
Reframe

How many engineers maintain your monitoring instead of your infrastructure, and what is that costing you?
