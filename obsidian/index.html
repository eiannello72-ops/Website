Where these deals stall

Six stalls I plan for from the first meeting, and the MEDDPICC checklist every POV and deal runs against.

"We already have a CASB"

I show the integrations and tokens a CASB never sees, using a read only connection to one app.

The POV keeps growing

I scope it to three apps and written success criteria, with a readout date agreed up front.

App owners push back

I bring the Salesforce and Workday owners in early, and show the connectors are read only.

Nobody owns AI agent risk

I find whoever chairs the AI council and make Obsidian the control they report on.

Our own security review

I send Obsidian's security package in discovery and pick the partner or marketplace path early.
