What I know about Pentera

The product, the buyer, and why this year matters.

The product

Pentera runs safe, real attacks across internal, external and cloud environments, agentless and in production. It shows which exposures an attacker could actually use, and the route they would take.

Pentera Resolve, from the DevOcean acquisition, turns validated findings into fixes and retests them.4

The buyer

The CISO signs, usually with the head of security operations, vulnerability management or offensive security as the champion.

Many deals run with a channel partner, and audit, risk and the board care most about the reports.

The moment

Pentera passed $100M ARR with 1,200+ customers, and added AI red teaming through the EVA acquisition.1,5

Gartner's CTEM framework made validation a named step in exposure management, and boards now ask for proof rather than scan counts.6

The conversation I would have with a CISO

Challenger meetings follow a set order. Step through the talk track I would bring to a CISO, with the evidence behind each step.

Warmer

Most security teams run a scanner, an EDR, a SIEM and an annual pentest. Each tool reports its own findings, and the backlog grows faster than the team can fix it.
What we establish
Tool sprawlEvery tool reports risk its own way
The backlogGrows faster than the team can close it

Reframe

The problem isn't finding vulnerabilities. It's knowing which ones an attacker can actually use. A severity score says what could happen, not what will happen in your environment.
The assumption we challenge
FromFix the highest severity first
ToFix what is proven exploitable first

Rational drowning

Here is how breaches actually start. We will swap these numbers for your own in discovery.
The data

22%Began with stolen credentials, still the top entry point7

Emotional impact

The board asks whether the company would survive a ransomware attack. The honest answer is probably, based on last year's pentest, and the environment has changed since.
Why it matters personally
CredibilityThe CISO answers the board with an assumption
BurnoutThe team patches around the clock and risk barely moves

A new way

Leading teams test continuously, the way an attacker would, safely in production. They fix what is proven exploitable first and retest to prove the fix worked.

ProvenExploitable in your environment, not theoretical

Running the deal

Challenger earns the first meeting and shapes the problem. MEDDPICC tracks what is proven and what is still missing. Every stage has exit criteria, and every deal has a mutual action plan the champion helped write.

  1. Month 1

    Insight meeting

    Teach the reframe to the CISO and agree that unvalidated exposure is worth fixing this year.

    Exit whenThe CISO names the problem, the cost and who else needs to be involved.

  2. Months 1 to 2

    Discovery and business case

    Map tools, pentest spend, backlog and audit needs with a Sales Engineer and the partner.

    Exit whenPain is priced, the economic buyer is known and the deal is registered with the partner.

  3. Months 2 to 3

    Proof of concept

    Scope, safety settings and testing approval agreed first, then a run in their environment against written success criteria.

    Exit whenCriteria are met and the champion has walked the CISO through the results.

  4. Months 3 to 5

    Security and procurement

    Security review, legal and purchasing run in parallel, timed to the pentest renewal and budget cycle.

    Exit whenPaper process is complete and a signature date is agreed in the mutual plan.

  5. Month 5 on

    Start where the proof was strongest, then add external, cloud and Resolve as the program grows.

    Exit whenSigned, with a rollout plan, a value review date and the next environments mapped.

    Take control

Where these deals stall, and what I do about it

A proof of concept with no finish line

Success criteria are written and agreed with the champion before the first test runs, so the readout ends in a decision.

Testing approval takes weeks

Running safe attacks in production needs change control and sign-off. I start that approval in week one, alongside scoping.

Budget sits in a services line

The business case moves pentest and consulting spend to a platform, built with the partner and the CISO's finance lead.

Only practitioners are involved

The CISO readout is booked at proof of concept kickoff, so the economic buyer sees the results firsthand.

The partner arrives late

Deals are registered with the partner at the first meeting, so pricing, paper and timing are aligned from the start.

The incumbent renewal slips past

The pentest and scanner renewal dates go into the mutual plan in discovery, and the close is timed to them.
