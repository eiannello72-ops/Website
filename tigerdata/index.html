What I know about Tiger Data

A short summary so we start from the same page.

The product

TimescaleDB turns Postgres into a high ingest, real time analytics engine with hypertables, columnar compression and continuous aggregates. Tiger Cloud runs it fully managed.

Agentic Postgres adds instant forks of production data, vector search and an MCP server, so AI agents can work on real data safely.2

The buyer

CTOs, VPs of Engineering and data platform leads, usually with a hands on engineer as the champion.

The strongest verticals are energy and utilities, oilfield services, manufacturing, data centers, crypto and AI native companies.

The moment

More than 2,000 customers, including Toyota, Siemens, Hugging Face and Mistral, with ARR growing over 100% a year.1

Snowflake bought Crunchy Data and Databricks bought Neon, and AWS closed Timestream to new customers. Postgres is where the data platform market is moving.6,7,5

The conversation I would have with a CTO

Challenger meetings follow a set order. This is the talk track for Tiger Data, in my words, with the evidence I would bring to each step.

  1. Step 1Warmer
    Most teams building on sensor, event or AI data start on Postgres. As they grow, they add a time series database, a warehouse and a vector store, each with its own pipeline.
  2. Step 2Reframe
    The expensive part of that stack is the pipelines and sync jobs between the databases. And the belief that Postgres can't handle real time scale is out of date.
  3. Step 3Rational drowning
    Here is what the sprawl costs a team like yours. We will swap these numbers for your own in discovery.
    4 to 1Plexigrid replaced four databases with one3350xfaster queries after the move3
  4. Step 4Emotional impact
    Think about the page at 2am when ingest stalls, the data engineer who spends half the week repairing pipelines, and the AI feature that slips because the data lives in four places.
  5. Step 5A new way
    The teams moving fastest run transactional, analytical and agentic work on one Postgres. They keep SQL and the ecosystem, scale to real time volumes, and let agents fork real data safely.
  6. Step 6Our solution
    Tiger Data delivers that with TimescaleDB, Tiger Cloud and Agentic Postgres.

Running the deal

Challenger earns the first meeting and shapes the problem. MEDDPICC keeps a three to nine month cycle honest and forecastable.

  1. Month 1

    Insight meeting

    Teach the reframe to the technical executive and agree the problem is worth solving this year.

    Teach
  2. Months 1 to 2

    Architecture and business case

    Map the stack with a Solutions Architect and price pipelines, infrastructure and engineer time.

    Tailor
  3. Months 2 to 4

    Benchmark

    Their workload, written success criteria, and an engineer on their side who owns it.

    Decision criteria
  4. Months 4 to 6

    Security and procurement

    Run in parallel, and ask early whether a cloud marketplace purchase can use existing commit.

    Paper process
  5. Month 6 on

    Close and expand

    Sign with a migration plan, a value review date and the next workloads identified.

    Take control
